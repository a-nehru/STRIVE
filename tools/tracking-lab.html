seen = frames with a detection. jitter = mean frame-to-frame move (lower is steadier; a still hand should read near 0). curl: about 2 open, 1 fist. CoV = bone length spread in % (a constant bone reads 0; for the ZED it is the 3D length in metres).

Use for the game

Saves the ZED settings shown above and the hand model to tools/tracking_config.json. The ZED bridge reads it when it starts, so restart py -3.12 tools\zed_bridge.py after saving. Flags on the bridge's command line still win.

Record for offline scoring

Full-resolution frames and every model's result go to tools/recordings/<stamp>/. Score one with py -3.12 tools\compare_models.py tools\recordings\<stamp> --hands mediapipe,wilor --body yolo,vitpose; replay it in the lab with --source rec:<dir>.
