# Music credits

All music in `assets/music/lofi/` is by **HoliznaCC0**, from the album
[Public Domain Lofi](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/public-domain-lofi)
on the Free Music Archive. Each track's page states it is licensed under
**CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), marked instrumental and
not AI-generated (checked 31 Oct 2026). CC0 asks for no attribution; it is
given here anyway, with thanks.

The files were re-encoded to 96 kbps MP3 and loudness-normalised to -20 LUFS
(ffmpeg `loudnorm`) so they all sit at the same level under the coach's
voice. Which game plays which track is set in `js/tracks.js`.
